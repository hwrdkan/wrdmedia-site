How I got here

  1. 2008

    I started WRD Media

    In Los Angeles, as a one-person video production company. Branded videos, documentaries, and commercials, start to finish. That is where I learned to run a project and make a client's message land.

  2. 2011

    Action sports and lifestyle brands

    I made branded content and ran social and YouTube channels for action sports brands. Fast turnaround, real audiences, and an eye for what people actually watch.

  3. 2014

    Directing in Taipei

    I scripted, storyboarded, and directed commercials, promos, and short films for a TV network in Taiwan. I speak English and Mandarin.

    I'm the COO of Agency Logics, and I run WRD Media as an AI-powered growth company. I use AI-assisted development to take a tool from idea to live, without a big engineering team or a long wait.

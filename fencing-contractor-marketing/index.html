Fence buyers compare on price because nothing else is clear

A fence looks simple, so buyers assume quotes should match, then see wide gaps and do not know why. The difference is usually material, height, post type, gates, and what is included. A contractor who explains those differences wins the buyer who is tired of guessing.

That means two things: answers to the questions people search, and a tool that lets them choose the options themselves and see the price move.

What changes the price of a fence

Fence quotes differ because of choices buyers cannot see:

  • Material: pine, cedar, metal, or masonry.
  • Height and style, and whether posts are wood or steel.
  • Gates, and how many.
  • Staining or sealing.
  • Removal and disposal of an old fence.
  • Slope, obstacles, and access to the site.

We do not publish a universal price table. Prices change, they vary by lot and design, and a table of averages would be a guess. The reliable number is a written quote for the specific project. A design tool built on your price sheet lets the buyer choose the options and see the range move.

Permit and HOA facts in the cities we serve

The Town of Prosper requires a fence permit unless you are replacing under 16 linear feet with like materials, and counts changes of more than 16 linear feet over twelve months. McKinney requires pool barrier fences to be at least four feet tall with self-closing, self-latching gates for pools 24 inches deep or more. For everything else, check the city and the HOA, because HOAs often limit fence height, material, color, and style more strictly than the city does.

Check before you build. Permit rules, fees, and HOA requirements change and vary by property. This page summarizes what cities have published. Confirm with your city and your HOA before work starts.

Common questions

How much does a privacy fence cost in Texas?

It depends on material, height, post type, gates, staining, old fence removal, and site conditions. We do not publish a universal price. Get at least three itemized local quotes and compare what each includes.

Do I need a permit for a fence in Prosper?

The Town of Prosper requires a fence permit unless you are replacing under 16 linear feet with like materials. Changes of more than 16 linear feet over twelve months need a permit. Confirm with the Town.

What fence is required around a pool in McKinney?

McKinney requires a barrier at least four feet tall around any pool, spa, or water feature 24 inches deep or more, with self-closing, self-latching gates and doors. Confirm with the city.

Can my HOA restrict my fence?

Yes, HOAs often restrict height, material, color, and style, and usually require architectural approval first. Check your declaration and apply before you order materials.

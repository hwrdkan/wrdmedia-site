Missed calls and lost leads: which numbers are real?

Contractors do lose business to unanswered calls, but the statistics you see online vary from about 28 percent to 62 percent of calls missed, and some of the most repeated numbers have weak sources. The best-supported finding is about caller behavior: in a 2025 CallRail survey of 1,000 U.S. consumers, 78 percent said they had abandoned a business after an unanswered call.

What this means for a contractor

The exact percentage does not matter as much as the pattern. You are on a roof or under a deck when the phone rings. If you do not answer, a large share of callers do not leave a message, and many move on to the next company. The cost is real even if no one can give you a precise number.

Measure your own, in one week

  1. Pull your phone's call log for the last 30 days.
  2. Count the calls you did not answer and the calls that came outside your working hours.
  3. Count how many of those you called back within an hour, and how many turned into jobs.
  4. Multiply the missed calls by your average job value and your usual close rate for a rough annual cost.

Practical fixes

  • Reply fast by text. A missed-call text-back that says you will call within the hour recovers some callers.
  • Put a booking link on your site and Google listing so people can self-schedule.
  • Use an AI receptionist for nights, weekends, and busy hours. See AI phone assistants.
  • Make sure your voicemail greeting names the business and says when you call back.

How to read statistics like these

Look for the sample size, who paid for the study, and whether voicemail counts as answered. A number from a company that sells the solution deserves a second look, including ours. That is why we show our sources.

A simple missed-call text-back

The cheapest improvement is replying quickly by text. Something like: "Hi, this is [name] at [business]. Sorry we missed your call. We are on a job. What do you need help with? We will call you back within the hour." Keep it short, name the business, and set a time. Avoid promising what you cannot do, and make sure the person can reply.

Common questions

What percent of small business calls go unanswered?

Estimates range from about 28 percent to 62 percent depending on the study. The widely cited 62 percent comes from a small 2016 study of 85 businesses that counted voicemail as unanswered.

Do callers leave voicemails?

A 2025 CallRail survey of 1,000 U.S. consumers found 42 percent say they leave a voicemail when a business does not answer.

How many customers do you lose from an unanswered call?

The same survey found 78 percent of consumers say they have abandoned a business after an unanswered call. Treat it as a survey result, not a precise loss rate for your business.

Is it true that 85 percent of callers never call back?

It is widely repeated, but one analysis found no primary source for it, so we do not rely on it.

What is the cheapest fix?

A fast text reply to missed calls and a booking link on your Google listing.

Should I answer every call myself?

You cannot while working. A fast text reply, a booking link, or an AI receptionist for after-hours calls are the realistic options.

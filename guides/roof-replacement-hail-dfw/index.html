Roof replacement after hail in Dallas-Fort Worth

Licensing: what Texas does and does not require

Several industry sources say Texas has no state roofing license, and the Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation, which licenses electricians and HVAC contractors, does not license roofers. Many cities require contractors to register, pull permits, or carry certain insurance, and the rules differ by city. The Roofing Contractors Association of Texas offers a voluntary license program, which is its own credential, not a government license.

Check before you build. Prices, permit rules, and laws change and vary by property. This guide summarizes published sources and is not legal or financial advice. Confirm with your city, your HOA, your insurer, and local contractors.

Insurance and storm claims, in plain terms

Reporting in the Dallas Morning News notes that only a licensed public insurance adjuster may work with an insurance company on a homeowner's behalf. A roofer can inspect, document, and bid, but should not negotiate your claim. Texas law also limits contractors from waiving or absorbing your deductible, so check the Texas Department of Insurance for current rules.

Many Texas policies set wind and hail deductibles as a percentage of the home's insured value rather than a flat amount, which can mean a large out-of-pocket cost per storm. Read your own policy for the percentage, deadlines, and what counts as covered damage.

What changes the price of a replacement

Roof price depends on the size and pitch of the roof, the shingle line, tear-off and disposal, decking repair, and flashing and ventilation work. Because storm-season demand changes pricing and availability, get two or three itemized written bids and compare what each one includes. We do not publish a universal price.

Class 4 impact-resistant shingles

Class 4 is the highest impact-resistance rating for roofing. Some insurers offer premium discounts for Class 4 roofs, but they are not required to, so ask your carrier before you spend extra. A claim of a specific discount percentage is the insurer's decision.

After a hail storm: sensible steps

  1. Photograph visible damage and keep dated records, then check your policy.
  2. Call your insurer to open a claim and ask about deadlines.
  3. Get two or three itemized bids from established local roofers, not just the first crew at your door.
  4. Check the contractor's city registration, insurance, references, and local address.
  5. Never sign a contract that hands over control of your claim, and do not agree to anything that waives your deductible.

Questions to ask a roofer

  • Are you registered in my city, and who pulls the permit?
  • Which shingle line and underlayment are you bidding, and what is the warranty?
  • Is tear-off, decking repair, and disposal included?
  • Can I see the roof in a visual mockup before I choose?
  • How long have you worked in this area, and can I see recent local jobs?

Common questions

Do roofers need a license in Texas?

Several industry sources say there is no state roofing license, and TDLR does not license roofers. Some cities require registration or permits. The RCAT credential is voluntary.

Can a roofer file or negotiate my insurance claim?

Reporting in the Dallas Morning News says only a licensed public insurance adjuster may work with an insurer on a homeowner's behalf. A roofer can inspect and bid. Check TDI for current rules.

What does a roof replacement cost in Collin County?

It depends on size, pitch, material, tear-off, decking repair, and the shingle line. Get two or three itemized written bids from established local roofers and compare what each includes.

Is a Class 4 roof worth it?

It is the highest impact rating. Some insurers discount premiums for it but are not required to, so ask your carrier first.

What should I do first after hail?

Document the damage, read your policy, call your insurer, and get itemized bids from established local roofers.

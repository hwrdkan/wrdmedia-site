What is GEO and AEO, and why does your business need it?

Generative engine optimization (GEO) is the practice of getting a page or business selected, quoted, and attributed inside answers from AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and Google's AI Overviews. Answer engine optimization (AEO) is the closely related practice of structuring content so search and AI systems can pull a direct answer from it.

GEO, AEO, and SEO: how they differ

SEO aims to rank your page in a list of links. AEO aims to have your content chosen as the direct answer. GEO aims to be named and cited inside a generated answer. In practice the three overlap, and the foundation is the same: accurate information, clearly written, on a site and listings that search engines already trust.

The metric shifts too. Instead of only asking "what position am I," you ask "am I named when someone asks for the best [trade] in [city], and by which assistants."

How AI assistants pick sources

Descriptions from industry guides are consistent. The system retrieves candidate pages, ranks them using relevance and trust signals, writes an answer, and cites the sources that best support it. Several guides add that assistants tend to pick passages, not whole pages, so a clear answer near the top of a well-structured section is easier to use than one buried in a long page.

Platforms differ. Perplexity shows its source links in every answer. Google's AI Overviews draw heavily on content Google already trusts. One analysis reported that, when ChatGPT searches the web, most of its cited pages overlapped with Bing's top results, so Bing visibility is worth checking too. Treat any specific percentage as directional, because vendor analyses vary.

What the research says

A late-2023 academic paper by researchers from Princeton, Georgia Tech, and IIT Delhi tested ways to make content more visible in generative answers. It reported that adding citations, statistics, and source attributions could raise visibility by up to about 40 percent in their tests. It is one study with its own setup, so it is a reason to be specific and sourced, not a promise of results.

What a local business can do

  1. Check what assistants say now. Ask the questions your buyers ask, in your city, in each assistant, and save the answers.
  2. Complete your Google listing. Services, hours, photos, service area, and real reviews.
  3. Keep your name, address, and phone identical everywhere. Your site, listings, and social profiles.
  4. Write specific answers. One page per real question, with the answer in the first two sentences and specifics after.
  5. Cite your sources. Link the city page, the manufacturer spec, the law. Facts with sources get quoted.
  6. Earn real mentions. Real reviews, directories, and genuine help in forums. Not fake reviews.
  7. Re-check monthly. Assistants change. Track whether you are named.

What does not work

Keyword stuffing, hidden text, fake reviews, and swapping the city name on a hundred copies of the same page. Assistants and search engines learn to ignore thin pages, and fake reviews can get you penalized. The plan that works is the slow, honest one.

Common questions

What is GEO?

Generative engine optimization is the practice of getting a page or business selected, quoted, and attributed inside answers from AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews.

What is AEO?

Answer engine optimization is structuring content so search and AI systems can pull a direct answer from it.

Is GEO different from SEO?

They overlap. SEO aims to rank links, AEO aims to be the direct answer, and GEO aims to be named inside a generated answer. The foundation of accurate, clear, trusted content is the same.

Can I guarantee ChatGPT recommends me?

No. Answers change and differ by assistant. You can improve the odds with a complete listing, specific sourced answers, consistent details, and real reviews, and you can measure the result.

How do I check if AI mentions my business?

Ask each assistant the questions your buyers ask, in your city, and save the answers. Repeat monthly.

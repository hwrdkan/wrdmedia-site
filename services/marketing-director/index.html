Architect: the CMO strategy and direction behind your marketing

A fractional CMO is a senior marketer who sets your strategy and directs the work part time, without a full-time salary. Architect is the strategy and direction layer of WRD Media's engagement: a 90-day roadmap, clear priorities, and oversight of your team and vendors, with a monthly report on what was done and what is next.

  • Included in your monthly engagement
  • 90-day initial commitment
  • 3 clients at a time

How is Architect offered?

Architect is not sold on its own. It is the strategy and direction layer of a fractional CMO engagement. Advisor is $4,999 a month and Embedded is $9,999 a month, with a 90-day initial term, then month to month. You keep everything we build.

What is included

  • A 90-day growth roadmap, refreshed every quarter.
  • Direction of your team and vendors.
  • Reviews of your offers and pricing.
  • Content and social direction.
  • Organic outreach strategy that drives inbound.
  • AI phone and text assistants, when they fit your business.
  • A monthly report: what was done, what it earned, and what is next.
  • In Embedded: live working sessions four times a week, continuous hands-on improvements, and the Ascend and Envision work run for you.

How it works

  1. Week one: strategy. We learn your business, your best customers, and your constraints, then set the priorities.
  2. Every week: direction. A check-in keeps the work pointed at the plan. In Embedded, hands-on work moves all week.
  3. Every month: report and talk. You get a clear report and live strategy calls to adjust.

Why a fractional director

Many local businesses do not have a marketing leader, so marketing can happen in bursts: a flurry of posts, then silence. Without direction, effort scatters and progress stalls. A director picks the few things that matter, makes sure they ship every week, and measures them. That is the difference between being busy and making progress.

Who it is for, and who it is not

It is for established local businesses with real revenue that want marketing run for them. It is not for brand-new businesses with no budget, or for owners who want to approve every post in real time. Because this is a hands-on service delivered by one person, the calendar holds only a few clients.

Where AI search fits in

Part of the work is making sure your site and listings are built so assistants can quote you. That uses the same methods as Ascend. If you only want that piece, Ascend is the smaller, cheaper way to start.

What the first 90 days look like

The 90-day commitment exists so the work has time to show up. Here is the shape of it. It is a plan, not a promise of specific results.

  1. Weeks 1 to 4: foundation. Strategy call, audit of your site, Google listing, and social profiles, a content calendar, and the first weekly improvements.
  2. Weeks 5 to 8: rhythm. Posting and engagement run on schedule, outreach starts, and the site gets answer-first pages that search engines and AI assistants can quote.
  3. Weeks 9 to 12: measure and adjust. The monthly report shows what moved. We double down on what works and drop what does not, and you decide whether to continue.

What it replaces

Many owners in this situation are choosing between hiring a marketing person, hiring an agency, or doing it themselves between jobs. A full-time marketing director is a major salary plus benefits, and a junior hire needs direction you may not have time to give. An agency often hands your account to someone junior. Architect puts one senior person, Howard Kan, on the work, with a clear cadence you can see.

What is not included

Paid advertising budgets and ad management are separate. Paid advertising is handled through preferred partner Agency Logics. Architect is the organic and strategic engine: content, outreach, website, search, and AI visibility.

Common questions

What is a fractional marketing director?

A senior marketer who runs your strategy and execution part time, so you get leadership-level direction without a full-time salary.

How is this priced?

WRD Media works as a fractional CMO, so Ascend, Envision, and Architect are included in the monthly engagement and are not sold separately. Advisor is $4,999 a month and Embedded is $9,999 a month, with a 90-day initial term. Details are at wrdmedia.com/services/.

What do I get each month?

A 90-day roadmap refreshed each quarter, two live strategy calls a week, a weekly check-in, direction of your team and vendors, offer and pricing reviews, and a monthly report. Embedded adds live working sessions four times a week, continuous hands-on improvements, the Ascend program run for you, and an Envision build in your roadmap.

How many clients do you take?

A maximum of 3 fractional clients, because the calendar only holds that many.

Is this an agency?

No. You work directly with Howard Kan, the founder. There are no account managers.

Do you run paid ads?

Not as part of Architect. Paid advertising is handled through preferred partner Agency Logics.

Is there a contract?

There is a 90-day commitment, then month to month. If the numbers do not justify it, you part ways and keep everything built.

Is Architect right for a brand-new business?

Usually not. It fits established local businesses with real revenue and something worth marketing. Book a free call and we will tell you honestly.

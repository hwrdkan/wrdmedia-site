Ascend: get named when buyers ask AI for the best in your city

How much does Ascend cost?

Ascend is a $3,000 one-time sprint plus $1,000 a month for monitoring and maintenance. You see the before and after screenshots of your AI answers, or you do not pay the balance. We take on 6 sprints per month because the audit alone takes real manual work.

What is included

  • We check where you show up, and where you do not, when people ask ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and Google.
  • We fix your Google listing: hours, services, photos, and reviews.
  • We clean up bad links pointing at your site.
  • We write the pages and answers AI quotes when it recommends a business like yours.
  • Before and after screenshots, so you can see the change.
  • Monthly proof you are showing up, plus a fresh check every month.

How it works

  1. Audit. We ask the assistants the questions your buyers ask, in your city, and record who they name today.
  2. Fix and publish. We repair your listing and links, then write specific answer pages in plain language that match how people ask.
  3. Monitor. Every month we re-run the questions, show you what changed, and keep improving.

Why this works the way it does

AI assistants retrieve candidate pages, weigh relevance, authority, freshness, and how clearly a page answers the question, and then quote the passages that best support the answer. A page that opens with a short, direct answer and backs it with specifics tends to be easier to quote than a long page that buries the point. A 2023 academic paper from researchers at Princeton, Georgia Tech, and IIT Delhi reported that adding citations, statistics, and source attributions can raise a page's visibility in AI-generated answers by up to about 40 percent in their tests. That is one study, and results vary by platform, but it matches what we do: be specific, be sourced, be consistent.

ChatGPT's search mode has been reported to overlap heavily with Bing's results, so Bing visibility matters too, not only Google. We check all of them. See our guides to what GEO and AEO are and how to get recommended by ChatGPT.

What we do not promise

We do not promise to make you number one, and nobody honest can. AI answers change from day to day and differ between assistants. What we promise is a documented audit, specific fixes, and visible before and after evidence. If we cannot show you a change, you do not pay the balance.

When it makes sense

  • You lose calls to competitors who show up when you search your own trade and city.
  • Your Google listing is incomplete, out of date, or has few reviews.
  • Your website says "quality work, free estimates" but does not answer any specific question.
  • You want to be early. Few local businesses are working on this yet.

What we need from you

Your business details, your service area, and access to your Google listing. And approval of our content within 48 hours.

What happens if you do nothing

Buyers keep asking assistants, and the assistants keep naming whoever has the clearest information. If that is never you, you lose those calls without ever knowing they existed. A single customer from AI often covers the entire sprint.

Common questions

What is GEO or AEO?

Generative engine optimization (GEO) and answer engine optimization (AEO) are the practice of getting a business named, quoted, and cited inside answers from AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and Google's AI Overviews.

How much does Ascend cost?

$3,000 one-time sprint plus $1,000 a month for monitoring and maintenance. If we cannot show a before and after change in your AI answers, you do not pay the balance.

Can you guarantee I will be recommended by ChatGPT?

No. AI answers vary and change. We guarantee an audit, specific fixes, and before and after screenshots, not a specific ranking.

How long until I see results?

The audit and fixes happen during the sprint. How fast assistants change depends on the platform and how much trusted information exists about you. Some changes appear in weeks, others take longer. We re-check monthly.

Does this replace SEO?

No. It builds on it. A complete Google listing, good reviews, clear pages, and clean links help both traditional search and AI answers.

What do you need from me?

Business details, your service area, access to your Google listing, and content approval within 48 hours.

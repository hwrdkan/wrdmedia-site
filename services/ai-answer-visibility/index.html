How is Ascend offered?

Ascend is not sold on its own. It is included in a fractional CMO engagement: run for you in the Embedded level and directed in the Advisor level. Advisor is $4,999 a month and Embedded is $9,999 a month, with a 90-day initial term, then month to month.

What is included

  • Website content revamp and structure: page hierarchy, internal linking, local city pages, and the guides and resources that answer real buyer questions, written with sources.
  • Google Business Profile: categories, services with descriptions, photos, hours, and service areas, kept accurate.
  • Regular posts: offers, updates, and news on your profile.
  • Consistent information: your name, address, phone, and details matched across your website, directories, and social profiles.
  • Citations and backlinks: checked so other websites and sources agree with your profile.
  • Monthly report: what we published, what we updated, and how your visibility is trending.
  • Periodic answer checks in ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and Google, so we can track progress honestly.

How it works

  1. Setup phase. We start with your website: a content revamp and structure optimization. Then we audit your Google Business Profile, listings, and backlinks, and fix and standardize them.
  2. Publish. New local content, services, and profile posts go out on a steady schedule.
  3. Report and refine. Each month you get a clear report, and we adjust based on what is moving.

Why authority comes first

Google and AI assistants favor businesses they can verify and trust: complete profiles, consistent information everywhere, and pages that answer the question clearly with sources. Descriptions of how assistants choose sources are consistent. They retrieve candidate pages, weigh relevance, authority, and freshness, and quote the passages that best support the answer. That is why the work is a profile, listings, and content, not a trick.

A 2023 academic paper from researchers at Princeton, Georgia Tech, and IIT Delhi reported that adding citations, statistics, and source attributions can raise a page's visibility in AI-generated answers in their tests. It is one study, so we treat it as a reason to be specific and sourced, not a promise. See our guides to what GEO and AEO are and how to get recommended by ChatGPT.

What to expect, and what we do not promise

Rankings and AI recommendations cannot be bought or switched on. They change from day to day, differ between assistants, and build over months. We do not promise a ranking, a number of calls, or a mention in any assistant. We do promise documented work every month and an honest report on where you stand.

When it makes sense

  • Your website has few pages that answer local questions, or its structure is hard to follow.
  • Your Google Business Profile is thin, outdated, or inconsistent with the rest of the web.
  • Competitors appear when you search your own trade and city.
  • You can invest for months, not weeks.

What we need from you

Your business details, service area, photos, and access to your Google Business Profile and website. And approval of our content within 48 hours.

What happens if you do nothing

Your competitors keep building authority while your profile and site stay static. It is a slow gap, not a sudden loss.

Common questions

What is GEO or AEO?

Generative engine optimization (GEO) and answer engine optimization (AEO) are the work of building the authority and clear information that help a business be found and cited in Google and in answers from ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity.

How is this priced?

WRD Media works as a fractional CMO, so Ascend, Envision, and Architect are included in the monthly engagement and are not sold separately. Advisor is $4,999 a month and Embedded is $9,999 a month, with a 90-day initial term. Details are at wrdmedia.com/services/.

Can you guarantee rankings or a mention in ChatGPT?

No. Rankings and AI answers vary, change often, and build over time. We commit to documented work each month and an honest progress report, not to a specific ranking or mention.

How long until I see results?

Plan on months, not days. Profile and listing fixes can help sooner, but authority and rankings build gradually. We report monthly so you can see the trend.

Does this replace SEO?

It includes it. A complete Google Business Profile, consistent listings, and strong local website content are the core of local SEO, and they also help in AI answers.

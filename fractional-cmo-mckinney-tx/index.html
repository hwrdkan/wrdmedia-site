The full answer

McKinney has an old downtown and fast new neighborhoods side by side, and the marketing that works for one does not work for the other. Howard Kan acts as a fractional CMO for McKinney trade and home-service businesses, setting the plan and directing the work.

What makes McKinney, TX a distinct marketing market?

McKinney is the county seat of Collin County, about 32 miles north of Dallas. The Census Bureau estimated about 207,500 residents in 2022, and newer private estimates run higher. Historic Downtown McKinney, with homes dating back to the mid-1800s, sits alongside large newer communities.

That mix matters for a contractor. A homeowner restoring a historic house and one finishing a new backyard are different buyers with different worries, and one generic message serves neither well.

What would a fractional CMO look at first in McKinney, TX?

  • Who you are actually selling to. Old-house owners and new-build owners ask different questions and need different proof.
  • Pool and fence content. The City of McKinney requires a barrier at least four feet tall around any pool, spa, or water feature 24 inches deep or more, with self-closing, self-latching gates. Explaining that clearly is useful content.
  • HOA approval. Many newer communities have HOAs with architectural review, which can be a slow step. Contractors who explain the process win trust.
  • Honest service-area pages. Name what you really did in McKinney. Thin pages are what search engines and AI assistants learn to ignore.
  • Offer and pricing review. Make sure what you promote matches what you want to sell.

What do McKinney, TX homeowners ask, and how should you answer?

McKinney homeowners ask who builds the best custom pergolas, what a pool costs and which fence is required, how to get HOA approval for a patio cover in Stonebridge Ranch, who installs turf and whether the HOA will allow it, and who replaces roofs after hail.

The checked detail lives on the McKinney page: McKinney: local rules and what homeowners ask. The CMO decides which questions to answer first and holds the work to a date.

What would the first 90 days focus on for McKinney, TX?

Month one separates the buyers: historic-district owners and new-build owners. Month two writes the roadmap around the pool, fence, and HOA questions each group asks. Month three publishes the first honest McKinney service pages and the first report.

How does the engagement work for a McKinney, TX business?

The engagement is the same everywhere: a free 20-minute strategy call, a written 90-day plan, then weekly execution and monthly reporting. Advisor ($4,999 a month) is senior direction for the team you already have; Embedded ($9,999 a month) adds live working sessions four times a week, continuous hands-on improvements, and the Ascend program run for you. We do not promise rankings or lead counts for McKinney, TX or anywhere else. We commit to documented work each month and an honest report.

Mistakes to avoid in McKinney, TX

  • Using one message for historic-district and new-build buyers.
  • Leaving pool barrier rules unexplained and losing the cautious buyer.
  • Paying for leads without a plan to answer them quickly.

What makes McKinney different

McKinney is the county seat of Collin County, about 32 miles north of Dallas. The Census Bureau estimated about 207,500 residents in 2022, and newer private estimates run higher. Unlike some of its neighbors, McKinney has two very different housing stories side by side.

Historic Downtown McKinney is one of the oldest downtowns in Texas, with homes and buildings dating back to the mid-1800s and roughly 1,400 historic buildings, according to the city's press kit. Around it are large master-planned communities such as Stonebridge Ranch and Craig Ranch, with newer homes, HOAs, and amenities.

That mix matters for contractors. Older homes bring roofing, fencing, porch, and remodeling work. Newer communities bring pools, patios, turf, outdoor kitchens, and pergolas. A single generic page cannot serve both buyers, so we write for each.

McKinney pool and fence rules worth explaining on your site

The City of McKinney requires a barrier around any private pool, spa, or water feature 24 inches deep or more. Pool barrier fences must be at least four feet tall, and gates and doors must be self-closing and self-latching. Above-ground pools follow the same rule as in-ground pools.

The city's pool permit packet asks for a pool plan, a fence detail showing how the barrier meets the rules, a survey, and approval from the utility company, and it notes that the pool cannot encroach on any easement and that HOA approval may be needed where an HOA exists. A plan review fee is also listed, and fees change, so check the current packet.

A pool or fence contractor who explains this clearly on their website saves the homeowner a surprise and makes the business the obvious expert. We turn this kind of verified detail into pages AI assistants can quote, with a link to the city's own page.

Common questions

Do you work with McKinney businesses even though you are in Frisco?

Yes. Frisco and McKinney are neighboring cities in Collin County. Howard Kan works with owners directly, by call, video, or in person when it helps.

What is the fence requirement around a pool in McKinney?

The City of McKinney requires a barrier around any private pool, spa, or water feature 24 inches deep or more. The barrier fence must be at least four feet tall, with self-closing, self-latching gates and doors. Confirm the current rules with the city before building.

Do I need HOA approval for a pergola or pool in McKinney?

If your property is in an HOA, its governing documents usually require architectural approval before you build. The City of McKinney's pool packet also notes that HOA approval may be needed. Check your declaration and apply to the HOA before you order materials.

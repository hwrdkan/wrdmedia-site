What does a fractional CMO do for a home-service business?

A CMO owns the question "where should the next marketing dollar and the next marketing hour go?" In a roofing, pergola, turf, fencing, pool, deck, or concrete business, that usually comes down to a handful of decisions: how you show up on Google and in AI answers, how fast you respond to a new lead, what your website and estimates say, which offers you promote, and who executes each piece.

A fractional CMO makes those decisions, writes them into a plan, and keeps the people doing the work pointed at it. You get senior judgment without a full-time executive salary. For the longer definition, read what a fractional CMO is.

What is included in the engagement?

Two levels. Advisor is senior direction for the team you already have. Embedded adds the work shipped every week.

  • Advisor, $4,999 a month: a 90-day growth roadmap refreshed each quarter, two live strategy calls a month, a weekly check-in, direct access with replies within one business day, direction of your team and vendors, reviews of your offers and pricing, one deep review a month of a page, funnel, or campaign, and a monthly report.
  • Embedded, $9,999 a month: everything in Advisor, plus a weekly working session, same-day priority access, one improvement shipped every week, the Ascend program run for you, an Envision 3D sales tool built into your roadmap, content engine direction, and hands-on team and vendor management.

Three named parts sit inside the engagement and are never sold separately: Ascend (AI visibility and SEO/GEO), Envision (3D sales tools), and Architect (CMO strategy and direction).

Who is it for, and who is it not for?

It fits established trade and home-service businesses that already sell, have a team or a few vendors, and need someone to set direction and hold the work to a plan. It is built for owners who are too busy running jobs to run marketing, and for businesses that have tried an agency or a marketing hire and still feel like they are guessing.

  • Not a fit if you are brand new and have not yet sold the work. A new business usually needs sales first, not a marketing leader.
  • Not a fit if you want a one-off task such as a logo or a single ad. The engagement is about direction over months.
  • Not a fit if you expect guaranteed rankings or lead counts. Nobody can honestly promise those, and we do not.

How does it work?

  1. Strategy call. 20 minutes. We look at where your marketing stands and what matters most. The call is free and is just a fit check.
  2. 90-day plan. You get a written roadmap with priorities, owners, and dates.
  3. Weekly execution, monthly reporting. Work moves every week. Each month you get a report on what was done, what it earned, and what is next.

Why does AI search change the job?

Buyers increasingly ask ChatGPT, Gemini, and Google's AI answers who to call, and those answers name only a few businesses. Getting named depends on authority: a complete Google Business Profile, consistent business information across the web, and pages that answer real questions with sources. That is the Ascend program, and it builds over months, not days. Read the plain-English version in what GEO and AEO are.

How does it compare with an agency or a full-time hire?

An agency usually executes tasks; a fractional CMO decides which tasks are worth doing. A full-time CMO is a permanent executive hire. Many trade businesses are between the two: past needing an agency to guess for them, not yet ready for a full-time executive. Common questions

What is a fractional CMO?

A senior marketing leader who works with a business part time on a retainer. They set strategy, choose priorities, direct the team and vendors, and report on results.

How is WRD Media priced?

WRD Media works as a fractional CMO. Advisor is $4,999 a month and Embedded is $9,999 a month, with a 90-day initial term and month to month after that. Ascend, Envision, and Architect are included in the engagement and are not sold separately.

How many clients do you take?

Three at a time, so each one gets real attention. When the three slots are full, new clients wait for an opening.

Is there a contract?

There is a 90-day initial term, then month to month. You keep everything we build.

Do you guarantee rankings or leads?

No. Rankings and AI recommendations change often and build over time. We commit to documented work each month and an honest report, not to a specific ranking or number of leads.

Who is this not for?

Brand-new businesses that have not sold yet, owners who want a single one-off task, and anyone who needs a guaranteed result.
